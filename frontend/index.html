    04Guardrails

    What the program refuses to do

    A compromised keeper should still be unable to move funds anywhere you did not approve.

    Enforced on-chain

    • Health factor is read from the lending obligation and oracle price, never trusted blindly.
    • Per-transaction and cumulative repay caps. Session keys expire.
    • A minimum-out bound on every swap.
    • CPIs only to whitelisted programs: Jupiter, Kamino, Marginfi.
    • No instruction that sends funds to a wallet outside your vault.

    Decided off-chain

    • The volatility forecast and the choice to act.
    • Yield routing, including whether the gain beats fees, tip and slippage.
    • Bundle assembly and Jito submission timing.
    • Nothing here can be proven on-chain, so the program never trusts it.

    05FAQ

    Questions

    Do you hold my funds?

    No. Your position sits under your own non-custodial vault PDA. The keeper can only call whitelisted instructions within the caps you set.

    What if the keeper is late or a bundle fails?

    Protection is best-effort. A slot is about 400 ms, oracles lag and bundles sometimes do not land. Pick a conservative trigger.

    Which protocols are supported?

    Planned: Kamino, Marginfi and Drift for protection; Meteora, liquid staking tokens and Jupiter Lend for yield routing.

    What does it cost?

    Network fees, Jito tips and swap slippage. A rebalance only runs when the expected gain exceeds those costs.
